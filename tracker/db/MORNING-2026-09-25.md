**One finding worth your attention** (OBSERVABILITY.md §9): `groupBy {region} (sumBy amount) sales`
is aggregated **in Ermine**, not in SQL. At tier s the database returns 388 rows for 8 regions, and
at m 7,701 rows for 12. At tier l this would dominate `DbFetchTopN`. The Trace view shows it as
`scanned 388` against `rows 8`, with the note "the database returned 388 rows; Ermine reduced them to 8".

**Second finding, FIXED in 2f (S2f-1, OBSERVABILITY.md §11):** every Highcharts chart in the panel, `Doc.SalesReport`'s
pie included, drew as a **black box**: the writers draw in Highcharts styled mode and the rules live in
`javafxwriter.css`, which WP-11 never loaded. Checklist E10 could never have passed. The panel now links it
as a fourth stylesheet, with `page.ts` countering its fixed-position and white-page rules; charts are capped
at 320 px. MEASURED headlessly in both themes at 1100 and 390 px: charts coloured, tables unchanged, no
console lines. If you saw black charts in your playtest, this was why.

**Engine-ticket candidate F-1** = NEW-3 below (`groupBy`/`sumBy` not pushed into SQL); `TraceReport`
shows it as a query reading 388 rows for 8 used. **FIXED 2026-09-26 (not reviewed, not committed):** the fixtures use
`aggregateByGroup`, one SQL GROUP BY; rows returned 388 -> 8 at tier s, documents byte-identical
(OBSERVABILITY.md §9 item 1).
